Rangers FC Leads Fan Experience Revolution

Three years after the Glasgow club bet on a new kind of digital matchday, supporters around the world are part of every game at Ibrox.

By our sports business correspondent in Glasgow
Ibrox Stadium, Glasgow. There is a seat here for every supporter now, wherever they are.
On a matchday in Govan the ground is full long before kick-off, as it has been for generations. What has changed is everyone who is not inside it. In 2026 the club’s digital presence was one website and one app, the same for every fan. Today a supporter in Toronto, New York or a few streets from Edmiston Drive follows the build-up, the match and the aftermath in a way shaped to them, and in the company of the people they watch with. The club calls it social, immersive and personalized. Its rivals call it the standard to beat.

The membership that behaves like a subscription

What began as a supporters’ scheme has become the way most fans reach the club: one membership, a direct relationship, and services that anticipate what each member wants.

2026

In 2026, the Rangers were among the first professional sports franchises to invest in a new kind of fan experience.

The team partnered with Supporter Labs, an MIT startup, to build and scale the AI infrastructure for the social, immersive, and personalized experiences that its fans enjoy today.

Before

In 2026, prior to working with Supporter Labs, the Rangers’ digital presence was a one-size-fits-all fan experience with static, one-way communication.

One website…one app…one experience for fans.

The Rangers website and app, 2026: news, fixtures, RangersTV, shop, tickets.

That digital fan experience provided very basic information focused on upcoming games, team news, merchandise and tickets.

It lacked data and intelligence about team supporters.

2029

Today, in 2029, after working with Supporter Labs, the Rangers’ digital presence is all about engaging fans in dynamic, surprising ways.

Rangers FC Membership Model

Fans take full advantage of their Rangers fan membership.

The Rangers were one of the first teams to build a fan membership business. It operates similar to the Amazon Prime membership model that anticipates the needs, wants and behaviors of its customers.

The Rangers FC membership creates a deeper connection between the Rangers and their fans. Through the membership, the Rangers establish a direct relationship with their fans and offer them a variety of services.

All personalized for how Rangers fans wanted to follow and engage with the team.

The starting point already exists: MyGers, the membership Rangers supporters hold today. In 2029 it is the way in to everything above.

  • The news and alerts the group asks for
  • Plans handled: who is in, where, when
  • Answers about the match, a player or the rules when someone tags the agent
First version
  • A small number of supporters’ groups, one agent per chat
  • The agent posts only what the group asks for or a plan needs
  • Nothing booked or paid for without approval
What we measure
  • Groups still active after two weeks
  • Share of each group that joins
  • Plans made in the chat; how often the agent is tagged
